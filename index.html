CASTAWAY MIMICS
top-down survival sandbox
Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, Space to use, E to interact, F1 for full controls.
Mobile: drag the left half to move; tap right-side buttons to attack, interact (E), place blueprints (F), open craft (C), cycle blueprints (B), or use mimic (M).
Audio is procedurally synthesised — turn on your sound and tap ♪ in-game to mute.
